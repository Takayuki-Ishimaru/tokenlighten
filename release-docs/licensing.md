## Permitted use

The intended policy permits, without charge:

- personal use;
- an individual's use of TokenLighten while performing work for an employer or client;
- personal, non-organizational redistribution, provided the distributor clearly states that TokenLighten is included and preserves the release's license and third-party notices; and

Organizations are welcome to let the maintainer know when they formally adopt TokenLighten, but notification is voluntary and is not a condition of the internal-use permission.
